# Exam

An **examination** (exam or test) is an <a href="assessment">educational assessment</a> intended to measure a test-taker's [knowledge](k), skill, aptitude, or classification in many other topics.

> Redirected from Test (assessment).

## History

- Exams date back to ancient China.
- The `imperial examination` was established in the Sui dynasty.
- Modern standardized testing spread in the 19th century.

<hr>

### Types

1. Formal: administered on paper, on a computer.
2. Informal: verbal or demonstrations.
